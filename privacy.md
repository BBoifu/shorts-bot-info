# Privacy Policy for shorts-bot

shorts-bot is a personal, hobby project that uploads short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses: only permission to upload videos to the channel of the person who signed in (the owner). It does not read, store or share any other data about the owner or about anyone else.

What it stores: a sign-in token, kept in the owner's private GitHub settings. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users: this app is not offered to anyone else.

Deleting access: the owner can remove the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: your-email@gmail.com
